| DEC-041 | 2026-08-19 | Make `checkoutGuard` fail open (not closed) when `StoreService.ensureLoaded()` times out without a confirmed reading | Accepted | Found while testing LAN/phone access: `ensureLoaded()` gives up after a 5s safety timeout, but `isOpen()` defaults to `false` until real data arrives — so a slow response (cold dev-server compile after a restart, a flaky connection) got misread as "confirmed closed" and blocked a real checkout attempt, even though the store was actually open. `ensureLoaded()` now returns whether the fetch actually completed; the guard only blocks on a *confirmed* closed reading (`loaded && !store.isOpen()`), not an unresolved one.
